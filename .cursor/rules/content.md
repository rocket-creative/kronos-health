# SKILL: Content & Copy
Triggers: copy, content, writing, headline, body text, brand voice

## Before Writing Anything

**Ask for the URL.** Get the source so text matches real brand voice.

If no URL exists, ask for:
- Existing marketing materials
- Competitor examples they like
- Tone descriptors

## Absolute Rules

### No Hyphens
| Wrong | Right |
|-------|-------|
| well-being | wellbeing |
| state-of-the-art | cutting edge |
| user-friendly | easy to use |
| 555-123-4567 | (555) 123 4567 |
| 9am-5pm | 9am to 5pm |

### No AI Phrases
Never use:
- "I'd be happy to..."
- "Let's dive into..."
- "It's important to note..."
- "Let's explore..."
- "Based on..."
- "Leverage" / "Utilize"
- "In order to" (use "to")
- "Due to the fact that" (use "because")
- "At the end of the day"
- "Synergy" / "Paradigm" / "Holistic"
- "World-class" / "Revolutionary"

### General
- Active voice, not passive
- Sentence case for headings
- Oxford comma always
- One space after periods
- Contractions OK (we're, you'll)

## Headlines

### What Works
Specific over vague:
- ✗ "Quality Healthcare Solutions"
- ✓ "Mental Health Care That Fits Your Schedule"

Benefit over feature:
- ✗ "Advanced Booking System"
- ✓ "Book Your Appointment in 60 Seconds"

Numbers add credibility:
- ✗ "Trusted by Many"
- ✓ "Trusted by 2,400+ Practices"

### Formulas
1. [Benefit] + [Timeframe]: "Get Matched in 48 Hours"
2. [Adjective] + [Noun] + [For Whom]: "Compassionate Care for Every Stage"
3. [Question]: "Ready to Feel Like Yourself Again?"

## Body Copy

- Mix short and long sentences
- One idea per paragraph
- 2-4 sentences per paragraph for web

### Transform AI Slop
Bad:
> We are proud to offer comprehensive healthcare solutions designed to meet the diverse needs of our valued patients.

Good:
> Healthcare that works for your life. Same week appointments. Providers who listen.

## Calls to Action

### Button Text
- ✗ "Submit" → ✓ "Book My Appointment"
- ✗ "Learn More" → ✓ "See How It Works"
- ✗ "Click Here" → ✓ "Download the Guide"

### Formulas
- [Verb] + [Benefit]: "Start Your Free Trial"
- [Verb] + [Possessive]: "Get My Quote"

## Industry Voice

| Industry | Voice |
|----------|-------|
| Healthcare | Warm, empathetic, not clinical |
| Biotech | Authoritative, precise, evidence based |
| Luxury | Understated, evocative, never oversells |
| Legal/Financial | Clear, confident, precise |

## Microcopy

Labels: "Email" not "Email Address"
Errors: "Please enter valid email" not "Invalid input"
Empty: "No appointments yet. Book your first one." not "No results"
Confirm: "Confirmed for Tuesday at 2pm" not "Success!"

## Checklist
- [ ] Asked for URL or materials
- [ ] No hyphens
- [ ] No AI phrases
- [ ] Active voice
- [ ] Specific, not generic
- [ ] Benefits before features
- [ ] CTAs action oriented
