## 3. Google Ads Strategy

### 3A. Google Ads Policy & Compliance Considerations

Before launching any campaigns, be aware of these critical compliance requirements:

**LegitScript Certification:**
- Google requires LegitScript Healthcare Merchant Certification for online pharmacies and telemedicine providers to run ads.[^17][^18]
- For TMS therapy specifically: TMS is an FDA-cleared medical procedure, **not** a controlled substance, so standard healthcare ads should be permissible without LegitScript. However, if Cognica offers any telemedicine consultations or online prescription components, LegitScript may be required.[^19]
- **For Spravato (esketamine):** This is a controlled substance (Schedule III). Google's healthcare policy restricts prescription drug advertising — advertisers generally cannot use prescription drug names in ad copy unless they hold LegitScript Healthcare Merchant Certification. **Recommendation: Apply for LegitScript certification proactively before the Spravato launch.**[^20][^21]

**Spravato REMS Compliance:**
- The Spravato REMS (Risk Evaluation and Mitigation Strategy) requires all healthcare settings to be certified before administering Spravato.[^22][^23]
- All patients must be enrolled in the REMS registry.[^24]
- Advertising should emphasize that treatment is supervised, in-clinic, and follows REMS protocols to build trust.

**Google Healthcare Policy Key Rules:**
- Cannot target users based on health conditions in personalized advertising.[^25]
- Can use predefined Google audiences (in-market segments, affinity, demographics) but **not** custom audience lists based on health data.[^25]
- Ads must not make misleading health claims.
- Landing pages must include clear provider credentials.

## 5. Landing Page Strategy

Every Google Ads campaign needs a dedicated landing page — **never** send paid traffic to the homepage.[^37][^38]

### Landing Page Best Practices for Cognica

Each service-specific landing page should follow this structure:[^39][^38][^37]

1. **Hero Section:** Clear headline addressing the patient's pain point (not the treatment name). Example: "Still Struggling with Depression After Trying Medication?" + subheadline: "FDA-Cleared TMS Therapy Is Helping Patients Find Relief"
2. **Trust bar:** Dr. Abrahams' credentials, NYP affiliation, insurance logos, "FDA-Cleared" badge
3. **How it works:** 3-step visual (Consult → Treatment → Results) with simple language
4. **Social proof:** Patient testimonials (video preferred), success statistics
5. **FAQ section:** Address top objections (cost, insurance, side effects, time commitment)
6. **CTA above the fold AND at bottom:** "Book Your Free Consultation" — phone number + form
7. **Mobile-optimized:** Over 60% of healthcare searches are mobile[^31]
8. **Fast load time:** Under 3 seconds
9. **No navigation menu:** Remove distractions — the only action should be booking[^39]

### Conversion Rate Benchmarks to Target

| Metric | Healthcare Average | Target for Cognica |
|---|---|---|
| Landing page conversion rate | 3–5% | 8–12% (optimized) |
| Click-through rate (Google Ads) | 3.27% average healthcare | 7–10% (with strong ad copy) [^40] |
| Cost per lead | $53 healthcare avg | $80–$120 (specialty TMS) [^29] |
| Lead-to-patient conversion | 30–60% insurance-based | 35–50% |

## 7. Social Media Ads (Supplementary to Google Ads)

### Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Ads

While Google Ads capture high-intent searchers, Meta ads are useful for awareness and retargeting:[^42]

**Best use cases for Cognica on Meta:**
- **Retargeting:** Show ads to people who visited the Cognica website but didn't book (install Meta Pixel on all pages)
- **Awareness campaigns:** Educational video content about TMS (what it's like, patient stories)
- **Lookalike audiences:** Based on existing patient email lists (once available)

**Meta limitations for Cognica:**
- Cannot target by health condition (HIPAA + Meta policy)[^31]
- Ketamine/Spravato content may face stricter review on Meta[^43]
- CPCs are much lower ($0.50–$2.00) but intent is also much lower[^29]

**Budget recommendation for Meta:** $500–$1,000/month max initially, focused on retargeting. Shift more budget here only after Google Ads campaigns are optimized and profitable.
