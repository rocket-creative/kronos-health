## Pre-Flight Checklist (Before Starting Any Part)

Before executing any of these parts, confirm the following:

- [ ] New Google Ads account is created and billing is set up

- [ ] Google Business Profiles are linked
- [ ] Conversion tracking tags are installed on iss.health and cognifica.health
- [ ] We are logged into the correct Google Ads account in the browser

**Google Ads Policy Compliance Rules (Apply to ALL Parts):**

1. **No unsubstantiated claims** — avoid "best," "guaranteed," "#1" unless backed by verifiable third-party data
2. **No personalized health targeting** — cannot target users based on health conditions
3. **Landing page must match display URL domain** — no redirects
4. **Healthcare certification required** for medical procedure ads
5. **No prescription drug names in ad copy** without LegitScript certification (affects Spravato ads for Cognifica)
6. **All headlines max 30 characters, all descriptions max 90 characters**
7. **RSA best practice:** 15 headlines + 4 descriptions per ad, pin sparingly, aim for "Good" or "Excellent" Ad Strength
