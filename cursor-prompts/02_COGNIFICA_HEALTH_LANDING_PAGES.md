**Google Ads Ad Group:** TMS Therapy
**Target Keywords:** TMS therapy near me, TMS treatment depression, transcranial magnetic stimulation, drug-free depression treatment

### Page Title

### Meta Description
"FDA-cleared TMS therapy for depression in West Harrison, Aquebogue, and Stamford. Non-invasive, drug-free treatment. Insurance accepted. Schedule your evaluation today."

### Hero
- **H1:** "TMS Therapy for Depression"
- **Subhead:** "FDA-cleared, non-invasive brain stimulation that treats depression without medication. Results in as little as 4-6 weeks."
- **CTA:** "Schedule Your TMS Evaluation"

### What Is TMS?
- Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) is an FDA-cleared treatment for major depressive disorder
- Non-invasive — no surgery, no anesthesia, no sedation
- Drug-free — no systemic side effects of antidepressants
- Sessions last 19-37 minutes, 5 days/week for 4-6 weeks
- NeuroStar TMS technology
- Can be combined with therapy and/or medication

### Who Is This For?
Patients who:
- Have been diagnosed with major depressive disorder
- Have tried one or more antidepressants without adequate relief
- Experience intolerable side effects from medications
- Want a non-drug treatment option
- Have treatment-resistant depression

### What to Expect
1. **Initial Consultation** — Meet with our care team for a comprehensive evaluation
2. **Treatment Planning** — Personalized TMS protocol based on your condition
3. **TMS Sessions** — Comfortable, outpatient sessions (you can drive yourself, return to normal activities)
4. **Progress Monitoring** — Regular assessments to track improvement
5. **Ongoing Care** — Maintenance sessions if needed, plus integrated support

### Patient Lifetime Value Context (for CPC justification)
- TMS: $8,000-$15,000+ per patient (mention internally, don't put on page)

### Competitive Note
- Aquebogue/East End Long Island has virtually zero local TMS competition — emphasize this location heavily in Long Island-targeted ad copy

**Google Ads Ad Group:** Neuropsychologist / Evaluation

### Page Title

"Comprehensive neuropsychological evaluations and cognitive assessments. Expert diagnosis for memory, attention, executive function, and behavior. Three NY/CT locations."

### Hero
- **H1:** "Neuropsychological Testing & Evaluations"
- **Subhead:** "Thorough cognitive assessments that deliver clear answers, detailed reports, and actionable treatment recommendations."
- **CTA:** "Schedule Your Evaluation"

### What Is Neuropsychological Testing?
- A comprehensive evaluation of cognitive abilities: memory, attention, language, executive function, processing speed, visuospatial skills
- Typically 3-6 hours of standardized testing
- Performed by trained neuropsychologists
- Results in a detailed written report with diagnosis and treatment recommendations
- Used for: diagnostic clarity, treatment planning, baseline measurement, medicolegal evaluations

### Who Needs Neuropsychological Testing?
- Individuals with suspected cognitive decline or dementia
- Patients recovering from concussion or TBI
- People experiencing unexplained memory loss or concentration difficulties
- Patients being evaluated for ADHD
- Individuals with neurological conditions (MS, Parkinson's, epilepsy, stroke)
- Workers' compensation and disability evaluations
- Pre-surgical cognitive baselines

### Our Testing Includes
- Standardized neuropsychological battery
- Automated digital evaluations for ongoing tracking
- Detailed written reports
- Treatment recommendations
- Referral coordination with your physicians
- Virtual evaluation options available

### What to Expect
1. **Referral or Self-Referral** — Contact us or have your doctor send a referral
2. **Intake Interview** — Review medical history, current concerns
3. **Testing Session** — 3-6 hours of standardized assessments (breaks provided)
4. **Report & Feedback** — Detailed written report + feedback session to discuss results and recommendations
5. **Treatment Plan** — Personalized plan based on findings, coordinated with your care team

### All Pages Must Have:
- Mobile-responsive design (Google Ads mobile quality is critical)
- Fast load time (< 3 seconds, test with PageSpeed Insights)
- Phone number (914-948-3008) as tap-to-call link
- Contact/booking form with Google Ads conversion tracking

- Schema.org MedicalProcedure markup (per service)
- Proper heading hierarchy (one H1, structured H2/H3)
- Alt text on all images
- Internal links to /team/, /conditions-we-treat/, and /consults/

### Google Ads Conversion Tracking
- Form submission = primary conversion
- Phone click = secondary conversion
- Install Google Ads tag and set up conversion actions
- Verify in Google Ads conversion diagnostics
- HIPAA note: Do NOT pass patient PII (names, conditions) through conversion pixels — track only the conversion event

### Compliance
- No Spravato or ketamine mentions in any ad copy or landing pages (no LegitScript certification)
- Healthcare advertiser certification must be verified before launch
- No claims about specific outcomes or cure rates without clinical evidence
