How It Works

A complete care pathway,
from screening to support.

01
Employee Completes Standardized Assessments
Employees access CognificaAI through a secure, confidential link via web or mobile. They complete a battery of validated clinical instruments — PHQ-9, GAD-7, PSQI, DAST-10, AUDIT, and PCL-5 — in under 10 minutes. No app download required. No IT integration needed.
02
Proprietary Risk Score Calculated Instantly
CognificaAI's algorithm synthesizes all six assessment scores into a single composite Risk Score — stratified into Low, Moderate, Elevated, and High categories. Each score is benchmarked against clinical thresholds established in peer-reviewed research.
03
Personalized Recommendations Delivered
Based on their risk profile, employees receive tailored guidance: psychoeducation for low-risk individuals, evidence-based self-help resources for moderate risk, and direct referrals to licensed mental health professionals for elevated and high-risk cases.
04
Crisis Support Available Around the Clock
Employees who screen at high risk — or who express suicidal ideation — are immediately connected to crisis resources, including 24/7 crisis line integration and automated notifications to clinical staff where appropriate and consented.
05
Employer Dashboard Tracks Aggregate Wellness
HR and wellness teams access an anonymized, HIPAA-compliant dashboard showing workforce mental health trends, engagement rates, risk distribution, and program utilization — without ever seeing individual employee data.

Clinical Instruments

Six validated tools.
One comprehensive picture.

Every assessment in CognificaAI is a gold-standard, peer-reviewed clinical instrument validated for use in both clinical and population-level screening contexts.

PHQ-9
Patient Health Questionnaire
The most widely used and validated tool for screening, monitoring, and measuring the severity of depression. Nine-item instrument mapped to DSM-5 diagnostic criteria.
Depression
GAD-7
Generalized Anxiety Disorder Scale
A seven-item instrument for identifying probable cases of generalized anxiety disorder and assessing symptom severity. Validated across diverse clinical populations.
Anxiety
PSQI
Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index
Assesses sleep quality and disturbances over a one-month interval. Widely used in both clinical and occupational health settings to identify sleep-related impairments.
Sleep Quality
DAST-10
Drug Abuse Screening Test
A ten-item brief screening instrument for identifying problems related to drug misuse. Sensitive, specific, and appropriate for use across all occupational settings.
Substance Use
AUDIT
Alcohol Use Disorders Identification Test
Developed by the WHO, the gold-standard tool for screening hazardous and harmful alcohol consumption. Identifies risk before dependence develops.
Alcohol Use
PCL-5
PTSD Checklist — DSM-5
A 20-item self-report measure assessing the presence and severity of PTSD symptoms. Mapped directly to DSM-5 diagnostic criteria for post-traumatic stress disorder.
Trauma / PTSD
Risk Stratification

From data to action —
automatically.

CognificaAI's proprietary risk algorithm synthesizes all assessment scores into a single, actionable risk tier. Each tier triggers a specific care pathway, ensuring no employee falls through the cracks.

Low Risk
All scores within normal range. Employee receives psychoeducation resources and wellness tips to maintain mental health proactively.
Wellness Content
Moderate Risk
One or more scores at subclinical elevation. Employee receives targeted self-help resources and is encouraged to speak with their provider or EAP.
EAP Guidance
Elevated Risk
Clinical thresholds exceeded on one or more instruments. Automated referral generated to a licensed mental health professional with direct booking.
Pro Referral
High Risk / Crisis
Severe scores or crisis indicators detected. Immediate connection to 24/7 crisis support, clinical escalation protocol activated, and urgent care coordination initiated.
