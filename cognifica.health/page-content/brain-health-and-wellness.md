## Our Brain Health & Wellness Program

Do you feel your cognitive sharpness isn't what it used to be? Even without an official diagnosis, it's common to sense changes in memory, focus, or overall cognitive well-being. Our Brain Health & Wellness Program is specifically designed to help you regain and maintain your mental edge.

## Program Components

### Cognitive & Emotional Evaluations
Detailed assessments conducted every 4 months to monitor and enhance your cognitive and emotional health.

### Initial Electroencephalography (EEG)
A precise baseline measurement of your brain's electrical activity for customized treatment.

### Computer-Based Cognitive Remediation
Interactive cognitive training sessions, personalized to your needs, scheduled 2–3 times weekly throughout the program.

### Daily Engagement & Progress Tracking
Access our proprietary software to effortlessly track your sleep patterns, diet, exercise, and overall wellness habits every day.

### Personalized Lifestyle Plans
Comprehensive programs for improving sleep, nutrition, and physical activity, complete with ongoing follow-up and support.

### Quarterly Neurofeedback Assessments
Periodic neurofeedback sessions to precisely measure progress and tailor your ongoing treatment.

## Key Features

### Stay Connected, Stay Supported
Our proprietary wellness software provides gentle prompts throughout the month, encouraging adherence to personalized sleep, diet, and exercise plans, ensuring consistent improvement and lasting results.

### No Long-Term Commitment Required
Your satisfaction and improvement are our highest priority. You have the freedom to cancel the program at any time, ensuring complete flexibility and peace of mind.
