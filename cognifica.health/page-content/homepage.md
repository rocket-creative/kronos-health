## Conditions We Treat

Cognifica is focused on providing the full complement of care to those patients who suffer from mental decline. We specialize in treating a wide range of conditions that can affect cognitive health, including:

- **Concussion & Traumatic Brain Injury:** Helping patients recover from head injuries and restore cognitive function.
- **Cognitive Dysfunction:** Targeted therapies aimed at improving memory, attention, and executive function to enhance daily living.
- **Chronic Pain:** Tailored treatment programs designed to reduce pain, improve quality of life, and empower self-management.
- **Anxiety:** Personalized strategies to manage anxiety symptoms, fostering resilience and improving daily function.
- **Depression:** Compassionate, evidence-based approaches combining modern therapies to help regain emotional balance and mental clarity.
- **PTSD:** Specialized care approaches that focus on healing trauma and supporting long-term mental health.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What can I expect during my first appointment at Cognifica?
Your first appointment typically includes an initial consultation to discuss your medical history, symptoms, and concerns. Depending on your needs, further tests or a comprehensive neuropsychological evaluation may be recommended.

### How long does a neuropsychological evaluation usually take?
A full neuropsychological evaluation typically takes between 3 to 6 hours. It involves a detailed interview, cognitive testing sessions, and a follow-up meeting to discuss your results and personalized treatment recommendations.

### Does Cognifica accept insurance?
Yes, Cognifica accepts various insurance plans, including Medicare, Tricare, Workers' Compensation, and No-Fault insurance. It's recommended to verify coverage with your provider before your visit.

### How can a neuropsychological evaluation help with post-COVID-19 cognitive issues?
Neuropsychological evaluations can identify and measure cognitive changes, including memory loss, concentration difficulties, and brain fog associated with COVID-19. Results help develop personalized treatment plans to aid in your recovery.

### Do you offer telehealth or virtual consultations?
Yes, Cognifica provides telehealth services, allowing convenient remote access to consultations and follow-up care. Contact your nearest office to schedule a virtual appointment.
