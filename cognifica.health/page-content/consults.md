## Contact Information Form

The consultation booking form collects the following information:
- Name
- Email Address
- Phone
- Address
- City/State/Zip
- Primary Care Physician
- Symptoms (multiple selection available)

## Available Symptoms for Selection

- Anxiety
- Adjustment difficulties
- Alzheimer's Disease
- Brain Fog
- Long Covid
- Menopause
- Movement Disorders (e.g., Parkinson's Disease)
- Seizures/Epilepsy
- Stroke
- Other
