### Positioning

"Intelligent Mental Health Screening and Risk Stratification for Employers"

CognificaAI is not an EAP (Employee Assistance Program). It is a clinically rigorous employer platform that proactively screens employees with validated instruments, stratifies their risk into tiers, and routes them to appropriate care — from self-guided resources all the way to 24/7 crisis support.

### The Market Problem (use this in copy)

- Mental health and substance use costs NY employers $79.4 billion in lost income — average $12,700 per affected worker over 5 years
- EAP utilization rates average 3-6% — 94-97% of employees with mental health needs go untreated
- 1 in 6 employees is experiencing moderate-to-severe anxiety at any time
- Employers typically learn about mental health issues only when employees quit or break down

### Clinical Instruments Used

| Assessment | Measures |
|-----------|----------|
| PHQ-9 | Depression |
| GAD-7 | Anxiety |
| PSQI | Sleep Quality |
| DAST-10 | Substance Use Risk |
| AUDIT | Alcohol Use |
| PCL-5 | PTSD Symptoms |

### Risk Stratification Tiers

| Level | Response |
|-------|---------|
| Low | Self-guided digital support, wellness content |
| Moderate | Targeted self-help, EAP referral |
| Elevated | Automated referral to licensed professional with direct booking |
| High / Crisis | Immediate 24/7 crisis support, clinical escalation activated |

### Key Differentiators (for copy)

1. 100% anonymous — employer never sees individual data, only aggregate trends
2. Clinically rigorous — 6 validated instruments, not wellness surveys
3. Real crisis protocols — active escalation, not just a hotline number
4. Therapist matching — connected to licensed professionals, not resource lists
5. HIPAA-compliant
6. Deploy in under 1 week — no IT lift required
7. Mobile-first design for frontline and non-desk workers
8. Built by clinicians (Dr. Abrahams and team)
9. 4x ROI for employers who invest in mental health programs

### Trust Stats (for copy)

- 1 in 5 U.S. adults affected by mental illness annually
- $1 trillion in lost productivity globally per year
- 46% of those with mental illness receive any treatment
- 4x ROI for employers who invest in mental health programs

### Messaging Rules (CognificaAI)

The biggest barrier from HR leader research: employees must see it as a benefit, not a monitoring tool.

- Always lead with employee benefit, not employer oversight
- Confidentiality is non-negotiable in messaging — lead with "private" and "anonymous"
- Frame the crisis protocol as a feature (real help, not just a hotline)
- Do NOT use language that implies employer surveillance of employees

**Say this:** "Helps your people get support before they hit a wall — completely private, completely theirs"
**Not this:** "Gives leadership visibility into team mental health"

**Say this:** "Understand what your workforce needs, without ever seeing individual data"
**Not this:** "Aggregated employee wellness data"

## 7. Shared Brand Rules

### Voice and Tone (All Entities)

- Evidence-based and precise
- Patient (or provider) benefit first — not the doctor's ego or the company's pride
- Active voice
- Sentence case for all headings (not Title Case Everywhere)
- Oxford comma always

### Things Never to Say (All Entities)

- "I'd be happy to" — AI giveaway phrase
- "dive into" — AI giveaway phrase
- "it's important to note" — AI giveaway phrase
- "let's explore" — AI giveaway phrase
- "leverage" — overused corporate filler
- "utilize" — use "use"
- "in order to" — use "to"
- "due to the fact that" — use "because"
- Hyphens in phone numbers — write as (914) 948 3008 not 914-948-3008
- "cure" for any condition — say "treatment," "management," or "may relieve"
- "best" or "#1" without third-party verification
- "world-renowned" without substantiation (this phrase is in existing copy — use carefully)
- Guaranteed outcomes — always qualify with statistics and context

### Healthcare Advertising Compliance Rules

These apply to Google Ads and any paid media:

1. No personalized/remarketing ads that target users by health condition
2. No unproven claims of cures for medical conditions
3. No "miracle cure" or "cure-all" language
4. Patient testimonials require a visible disclaimer: "Results vary and are not guaranteed"
5. Any cited statistics must be verifiable
6. Display URL domain must exactly match the final landing page domain
7. Do NOT send iss.health or cognifica.health ad traffic to any other domain
8. Spravato/ketamine ads require LegitScript certification — do not run until obtained
9. No PHI (patient names, conditions, phone numbers) through conversion tracking pixels
10. Healthcare certifier certification required in Google Ads account 393-266-9621

### Banned Domains (Never Reference in Any Ad or Copy)

These domains are compromised or have serious policy violations:
- cervical-center.com
- lumbar-center.com
- nybrainspine.com
- cognitive-centers.com
