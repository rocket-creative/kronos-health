**MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT**

Marketing & Brand Management Services

This Master Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of February 16, 2026 ("Effective Date") by and between:

**ROCKET CREATIVE LLC / UXUI DESIGN CORP** ("Provider"), a New York limited liability company, with principal place of business at 2107 Mill Rd, Ronkonkoma, NY 11779

And

**DR. JOHN M. ABRAHAMS, M.D.** ("Client"), on behalf of Institute for Spine Surgery (ISS), Cognica Health, and Kronos Health (collectively, the "Companies").

**1\. SERVICES**

Provider agrees to perform marketing and brand management services for the Companies as described in the attached Statement of Work (Exhibit A). Services shall include social media management, Google Adwords management, newsletter creation, website updates, direct provider outreach, and related marketing activities.

**2\. TERM AND TERMINATION**

**2.1 Term.** This Agreement shall commence on February 16, 2026 and continue on a month to month basis until terminated by either party.

**2.2 Termination for Convenience.** Either party may terminate this Agreement with thirty (30) days written notice.

**2.3 Termination for Cause.** Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately for material breach that remains uncured for fifteen (15) days after written notice.

**2.4 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination, Client shall pay for all services performed through the termination date. All work product shall be delivered upon final payment.

**3\. COMPENSATION**

**3.1 Monthly Retainer.** Client agrees to pay Provider a monthly retainer of Seven Thousand Five Hundred Dollars ($7,500.00) for all services across all three Companies.

**3.2 Billing Cycle.** Invoices are issued on the 1st of each month for the upcoming service period and are due by the 16th of the same month. First invoice is due upon execution of this Agreement.

**3.3 Payment Method.** Payment shall be made via Stripe.

**3.4 Late Payment.** Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month (18% annualized). Provider reserves the right to suspend services for invoices more than fifteen (15) days overdue.

**3.5 Sales Tax.** Services provided hereunder are professional marketing services and are not subject to New York State sales tax.

**4\. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY**

**4.1 Work Product.** All work product created by Provider specifically for Client under this Agreement shall become the property of Client upon full payment ("Work Product"). Provider hereby assigns to Client all right, title, and interest in and to the Work Product.

**4.2 Provider Materials.** Provider retains ownership of its pre-existing methodologies, frameworks, tools, templates, and proprietary technology developed prior to or independent of this Agreement.

**4.3 Portfolio Rights.** Provider reserves the right to use anonymized performance data, campaign results, and creative work for case studies and portfolio purposes, subject to Client approval.

**5\. CONFIDENTIALITY**

Both parties agree to protect and keep confidential all non-public information disclosed during the course of the engagement, including but not limited to trade secrets, business plans, patient information, proprietary methodologies, and financial data. This obligation shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years.

**6\. HIPAA COMPLIANCE**

Provider acknowledges that Client operates in healthcare and may be subject to the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act ("HIPAA"). Provider agrees not to access, use, or disclose any Protected Health Information ("PHI") unless specifically authorized in writing by Client. If Provider is required to access PHI, the parties shall execute a separate Business Associate Agreement prior to any such access.

**7\. NON-COMPETE AND CONFLICT OF INTEREST**

**7.1 Acknowledgment.** Both parties acknowledge that Provider serves multiple clients in the healthcare industry, including other medical practices. Client understands and agrees that Provider is not precluded from providing marketing services to other healthcare providers, including specialists in related fields, provided such engagement does not create a direct conflict of interest.

**7.2 Direct Competitors.** Provider agrees to notify Client in writing before accepting any engagement with a Direct Competitor. For purposes of this Agreement, "Direct Competitor" means any neurosurgery, spine surgery, or mental health practice located within a twenty (20) mile radius of any of Client's practice locations that offers substantially similar services to those offered by ISS, Cognica Health, or Kronos Health.

**7.3 Client Consent.** Upon receiving notice of a potential Direct Competitor engagement, Client shall have ten (10) business days to object in writing. If Client objects, Provider and Client shall meet to discuss the potential conflict and determine whether appropriate safeguards (such as information barriers or separate account teams) can be implemented. If no mutually acceptable resolution can be reached, Provider may decline the new engagement or, at Provider's option, either party may terminate this Agreement with thirty (30) days notice.

**7.4 Information Barriers.** If Provider serves multiple healthcare clients, Provider agrees to maintain appropriate information barriers to ensure that confidential information, marketing strategies, and competitive intelligence from one client is not shared with or used to benefit another client.

**7.5 No Exclusivity.** Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to grant Client exclusive rights to Provider's services in any industry, specialty, or geographic area, except as expressly provided in Section 7.2.

**8\. INDEMNIFICATION**

**8.1 Indemnification by Provider.** Provider shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Client and its officers, directors, employees, and agents from and against any and all claims, damages, losses, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or related to: (a) Provider's breach of this Agreement; (b) Provider's negligence or willful misconduct; (c) any claim that the Work Product infringes or misappropriates any third party intellectual property rights; or (d) Provider's violation of any applicable law or regulation.

**8.2 Indemnification by Client.** Client shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Provider and its officers, directors, employees, and agents from and against any and all claims, damages, losses, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or related to: (a) Client's breach of this Agreement; (b) Client's negligence or willful misconduct; (c) any content, materials, or information provided by Client for use in the Services; (d) any claims related to medical malpractice or patient care; or (e) Client's violation of any applicable law or regulation.
