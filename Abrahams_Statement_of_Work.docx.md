**STATEMENT OF WORK**

Exhibit A to Master Service Agreement

**PROJECT OVERVIEW**

Provider shall deliver comprehensive marketing and brand management services for three (3) healthcare and technology companies: Institute for Spine Surgery (ISS), Cognica Health, and Kronos Health.

**COMPANIES COVERED**

| Company | Focus | Time Allocation |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Institute for Spine Surgery (ISS) | Surgical practice marketing, referral development | 25% |
| Cognica Health | TMS, Spravato, Concussion, Mental Health services | 50% |
| Kronos Health | Revenue cycle services, software | 25% |

**DELIVERABLES**

**Social Media Management**

* 2 to 3 posts per week per company (minimum 6 to 9 total posts weekly)  
* Content creation including graphics, copy, and hashtag strategy  
* LinkedIn page updates and optimization for all three companies  
* Engagement monitoring and response guidance  
* Monthly performance reporting

**Google Adwords Management**

* Campaign setup, optimization, and ongoing management  
* Keyword research and bid management  
* Ad copy creation and A/B testing  
* Monthly performance reporting and budget recommendations  
* Note: Ad spend is separate and billed directly by Google to Client

**Monthly Newsletters**

* One (1) newsletter per company per month (3 total)  
* Content development, design, and distribution  
* List management and segmentation  
* Open rate and click through reporting

**Quarterly Website Updates**

* Content updates and refresh for all three company websites  
* New page creation as needed  
* SEO optimization  
* Performance and analytics review

**Direct Provider Marketing**

* Development of outreach materials for local healthcare providers  
* Targeted marketing for TMS, Spravato, Concussion, and surgical referrals  
* Coordination with Client team on outreach strategy

**COMMUNICATION AND REPORTING**

* Weekly team call: Mondays at 9:00 AM (starting February 23, 2026\)  
* Weekly status updates detailing posts, statistics, and responses  
* Monthly performance reports with analytics and recommendations  
* Quarterly strategy review and planning sessions

**CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES**

* Provide access to all social media accounts, website backends, and Google Adwords accounts  
* Designate point of contact for content approvals (Dr. Abrahams initially, with Juliet as backup)  
* Review and approve content within 48 business hours  
* Provide subject matter expertise and review for scientific/medical accuracy  
* Participate in weekly calls and quarterly planning sessions

**COMPENSATION**

| Description | Amount |
| :---- | :---- |
| Monthly Retainer (all services, all three companies) | $7,500.00 |
| Annual Total | $90,000.00 |

This retainer covers all services listed above. Invoices are issued on the 1st of each month and due by the 16th. Google Adwords spend is separate and billed directly by Google. Any services outside this scope will be quoted separately.

**START DATE**

February 16, 2026
