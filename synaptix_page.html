Visit Protocol

Three visit types.
One standardized system.

Synaptix structures concussion management into three defined visit types — each with its own form requirements, testing components, and automatically assembled clinical report.

Initial Evaluation (IE)
// First visit · Occurs one time

The initial evaluation establishes the complete clinical baseline. The patient completes a comprehensive concussion history while the physician performs a neurological and vestibular exam, entering all findings into Synaptix's structured forms. Neuropsychological testing is administered at this visit.

RequiredHistory (HX) — Completed by patient
RequiredExam (EX) — Physician completes; defaults pre-populated
TestingNPE-CX — Neuropsychological assessment battery
RequiredDiagnosis (DX) — ICD-10 selection with secondary codes
RequiredPlan of Care (POC) — Trigger-based functional recommendations
IE Report Assembly
HX + EX + DX + POC
Report is automatically assembled from completed form components. NPE results administered at IE are carried forward to the Feedback Visit report.
Feedback Visit (FB)
// Second visit · Occurs one time · ~2 weeks post-IE

The feedback visit is where the physician reviews all initial evaluation results with the patient and establishes the treatment plan. The exam from the IE is carried forward. NPE results — administered at the IE — are formally reviewed and interpreted at this visit.

RequiredHistory (HX) — Carried from IE visit
RequiredExam (EX) — Carried from IE; re-examined if indicated
TestingNPE Results — Interpreted at FB; administered at IE
OptionalDigital Evaluations (DE) — Included if available
RequiredDiagnosis (DX) + Plan of Care (POC)
FB Report Assembly
HX + EXIE + NPE + DE? + DX + POC
EX is the original IE exam, carried forward. DE inclusion is optional — added only if digital evaluations were completed between IE and FB. NPE is interpreted and formally documented here.
Re-Evaluation (RE)
// Recurring · Every 12 weeks · May occur multiple times

The re-evaluation is the program's longitudinal checkpoint. It includes an updated patient history, all NPE assessments since the last Feedback Visit, a full CRT session summary with trends, and cumulative digital evaluation data — providing a comprehensive picture of recovery progress.

RequiredHistory (HX) — Original
RequiredHistory Update (HU) — Patient completes at RE visit
OptionalExam (EX) — Re-administered at physician discretion
TestingAll NPEs since FB date — Scores + trends
TestingDE Summary — Sessions, scores, trends
TestingCRT Summary — Sessions completed, score trends
RequiredDiagnosis (DX) + Updated Plan of Care (POC)
RE Report Assembly
HX + HU + EX? + NPE×n + DE + CRT + DX + POC
All NPE sessions after the FB date are included. CRT and DE summaries include session counts, composite scores, and trend analysis over the 12-week cycle.
Proprietary Algorithm

Clinical logic,
encoded and automated.

01 //
Structured History Collection
Synaptix guides patients through a 21-section concussion history capturing injury mechanism, loss of consciousness, immediate symptoms, prior concussions, medical history, and full symptom scoring across 11 domains on a 0–10 scale.
02 //
Exam with Intelligent Defaults
The physical examination — covering neurological baseline, vestibular function (VOR, VMS, NPC), smooth pursuits, saccades, and BESS balance testing — pre-populates with normal defaults. Physicians document only deviations, reducing documentation time by over 60%.
03 //
Neuropsychological Battery Processing
PCSS, HIT-6, PHQ-9, GAD-7, PCL-5, and PSQI scores are synthesized against validated clinical thresholds. The algorithm identifies score combinations that trigger specific clinical interventions, automatically generating a customized Plan of Care.
04 //
Trigger-Based Plan of Care Generation
Each symptom score combination triggers a pre-defined functional recommendation — work restrictions, driving limitations, screen time guidelines, vestibular PT referrals, cognitive remediation therapy, and return-to-clinic scheduling — assembled automatically into the Plan of Care.
05 //
Longitudinal Outcomes Tracking
All NPE, CRT, and Digital Evaluation data is stored longitudinally per patient. The RE report automatically aggregates all data since the Feedback Visit, generating trend analyses, score trajectories, and session summaries without manual data entry.

Neuropsychological Battery

Six instruments.
Complete cognitive picture.

The NPE-CX battery combines the gold-standard instruments for concussion-related cognitive and psychological assessment — administered digitally, scored automatically, and compared against normative clinical thresholds.

PCSS
Post-Concussion Symptom Scale
22-symptom self-report scale validating concussion symptom burden. Threshold >27 triggers CRT enrollment. Tracked at every NPE session for longitudinal trend analysis.
Monthly / Every visit
HIT-6
Headache Impact Test
Measures the impact of headaches on daily functioning. Score >59 indicates severe headache impact and triggers cognitive remediation referral alongside PCSS.
Monthly
PHQ-9
Patient Health Questionnaire
Depression severity screening. Score >10 triggers CognificaChat referral (2×/week × 12 weeks). Monitored across visits for treatment response evaluation.
Monthly
GAD-7
Generalized Anxiety Disorder Scale
Anxiety severity measure. Score >10 co-triggers mental health referral with PHQ-9. Distinguishes anxiety-related cognitive complaints from direct concussion effects.
Monthly
PCL-5
PTSD Checklist — DSM-5
Trauma symptom assessment critical for patients with assault or MVA-related concussions. Score >21 triggers CognificaChat referral protocol for trauma-focused care.
Monthly
PSQI
Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index
Sleep quality assessment essential in concussion management, where sleep disturbance both signals and perpetuates neurological impairment and impedes recovery.
Monthly
