## 2. Kronos Health — Business Units & Services

Kronos Health is an umbrella company with four core service lines plus two technology products:

### Service Line 1: Payment Dispute Resolution (Revenue Cycle)

Expert arbitration and out-of-network dispute resolution support under the No Surprises Act.

**What they do:**
- Complete Case Management — end-to-end IDR process handling
- Compelling Submissions — preparing payment offers with clinical insight
- Deadline & Compliance Tracking — managing all federal requirements
- Post-Arbitration Follow-Up — ensuring favorable outcomes

**How it works (4-step process):**
1. Submit Negotiation
2. Initiate IDR
3. Build the Case
4. Arbitration

**Value props:**
- We Work for Providers (not insurers)
- We Know the Law
- We Maximize Reimbursement
- Transparent pricing with fee breakdown

**Team — Revenue Cycle:**
- Heisha Rivera — Director of Revenue Cycle (20+ years, expert in No Surprises Act)
- Camila Nicasio — Revenue Cycle Specialist
- Nicole Cedeno — Revenue Cycle Specialist (5+ years)
- Soily Rivera — Revenue Cycle Specialist (22+ years)

### Service Line 2: Cognitive Franchise Program

A low-cost, scalable franchise model for treating concussions, chronic pain, and cognitive decline.

**Key details:**
- 16 million Americans over 50 living with undiagnosed cognitive dysfunction
- 1.7 million TBI cases annually
- Quick adoption, minimal startup costs
- One dedicated provider per franchise
- Rising franchise fee structure for long-term growth
- Non-drug therapies emphasized

**Core Focus Areas:**
- Concussion
- Chronic Pain
- Cognitive Dysfunction

**Clinical Approach:**
- Neuropsychological Standard Assessments
- Initial Evaluation & Testing (9-item battery)
- Cognitive Therapy & Reassessment (4-item program)

### Service Line 3: Digital Health Tools

Turnkey tools for providers to track patient outcomes using existing CMS CPT codes.

**Specialties covered:** Orthopedics, Neurosurgery, Neurology, Trauma, Cardiology, Physiatry

**Three products:**
1. **Tappy** — SMS-based cognitive health assistant
2. **AISA** — AI spine recovery guide
3. **Digital Evaluation Module** — Automated CPT-coded reports for streamlined reimbursement

**Turnkey services:** Enrollment, data review, note generation, EMR submission

### Service Line 4: Clinically Integrated Neuropsychology

A referral network connecting physicians treating high-risk patients with specialty mental health providers.

**How it works:**
1. In-Office Screening — physicians screen patients using validated tools
2. Streamlined Referral Process — connect to Kronos Health network
3. Enhanced Patient Outcomes — coordinated care
4. Provider Collaboration — shared care plans

**CPT Codes for Screening:** 96127, 96116, 96130, 96131, 96132, 96133

### Synaptix — Concussion Assessment & Recovery Platform

**Branding:** "Synaptix — Cognitive Software" / "Software Concussion Management Tool"
**Tagline:** "Structured. Billable. Recurring."

Comprehensive concussion management system combining clinical history, neurological examination, and neuropsychological testing.

**Target Markets:** Orthopedic, neurosurgery, and sports medicine practices

**Sales Pitch (from brochure):**
- Capture post-injury cases already entering your practice
- Build a structured 12-week recovery program that generates recurring, billable revenue
- **Annual Revenue Potential: $260K** (with one new patient added each week)
- **$1,500 to $5,000 per patient each year**
- **Low Start-Up Costs:** Provider (MD/DO/NP) + computer workstation
- **12-Week Cycle · 3 Billable Visits · Zero Added Staff · No Referrals Lost**

**Practice Impact:**
- Recurring patient engagement
- Concussion specialty service line
- Expanded scope without added staff
- Stronger compliance and documentation

**Licensing Model:**
- 30-day trial from existing patients
- Monthly license by volume
- Short training required

**Core Components:**
- NPE-CX (Neuropsychological Assessment) — uses PHQ-9, GAD-7, PSQI, HIT-6, PCSS, PCL-5
- Digital Evaluations — weekly SMS/email symptom surveys
- Computerized Remediation Therapy — brain games based on Montreal Cognitive Assessment, 1-2x/week

**Clinical Workflow:**

#### Initial Visit
- History & physical + concussion-focused clinical exam
- Neuropsychological Testing (NPE-CX)
- Enroll in Digital Evaluations
- **Coding:** E&M (99214-99215) + NPE Testing (96136 + 96137)
- If insurance won't accept 96136/96137 from non-neuropsychologist: use 96116 + 96121

#### Plan of Care Thresholds
- **Headache > 4:** Limit work to part-time, limit driving, no heavy lifting, screen time < 30 min/day
- **Dizziness > 4:** No work, no driving, no heavy lifting, screen time < 30 min/day, start vestibular PT
- **Brain Fog > 4:** No work, no driving, no heavy lifting, screen time < 60 min/day

#### Feedback Visit (2 weeks after initial)
- Review Neuropsychological Testing Results
- Develop treatment plan
- **Coding:** NPE Interpretation (96132 + 96133); alt codes 96116 + 96121
- If HIT-6 > 50, PCSS > 39, or clinical symptoms suggest concussion → enroll in Computerized Cognitive Remediation 1-2x/week for 12 weeks
- If PHQ-9 > 10 or GAD-7 > 10 or PCL-5 > 21 → referral to mental health specialist or CognificaChat
- Enable weekly digital check-ins
- Monthly NPE-CX assessments
- Return in 12 weeks for re-evaluation

#### Weekly Digital Check-Ins
- **Coding:** 99421 (5-10 min), 99422 (11-20 min), 99423 (>20 min); alt code 96146

#### Computer Remediation Therapy
- **Coding:** 96158 (Individual Behavioral Intervention, 30 min) + 96159 (add'l 15 min)

#### Re-Evaluation Visit (every 12 weeks)
- History update + exam + NPE-CX
- Review: NPE assessments, weekly check-ins, CRT results
- **Coding:** Same as feedback visit + testing codes
- Continue cycle until symptoms subside

#### Clinical Example
35-year-old MVA patient with concussion, severe headache, dizziness, irritability:
- Initial: 99215 + 96136 + 96137 → Rest, no work, reduce screen time 2 weeks
- Feedback: 96132 + 96133 → Diagnosed concussion w/o LOC, enroll in CRT + digital evals
- Monthly: Digital Eval 99421 + CRT 96158/96159 x2 + NPE-CX 96136/96132
- Re-eval at 3 months: 99215 + 96136 + 96137 → Continue cycle
