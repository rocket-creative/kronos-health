## Overview

Kronos Health offers innovative, easy-to-implement solutions that leverage existing CMS CPT codes to track patient outcomes across multiple specialties, including:

- Orthopedics
- Neurosurgery
- Neurology
- Trauma
- Cardiology
- Physiatry

Our digital health services provide full support—from patient enrollment to data collection and submission—ensuring seamless provider workflows and CPT billing readiness.

## Turnkey Services

- Patient enrollment and engagement
- Ongoing data review
- Automated note generation
- Submission to providers for EMR integration and billing

## Our Solutions

### Cognitive Health – Tappy

Tappy is a proprietary SMS-based tool that interacts with patients through their mobile phones to collect essential cognitive health data.

- Integrates directly with provider EMRs
- Streamlines CPT billing through automated note delivery
- Enhances patient engagement with minimal effort

### Spine Outcomes – AISA

AISA (Artificial Intelligent Spine Assistant) guides patients through their post-operative recovery journey with structured check-ins and support.

- Adaptable for any specialty or condition
- Helps ensure better compliance and trackable outcomes

### Digital Evaluation Module

This software utilizes CPT codes for digital health to ensure proper reimbursement based on outcomes.

- Uses SMS-based technology for broad accessibility
- Automates documentation and billing preparation for providers
