## Arbitration & Out-of-Network Dispute Resolution

### Expert Support for Navigating the No Surprises Act

When a healthcare provider and an insurance company can't agree on payment for out-of-network services, the No Surprises Act's Independent Dispute Resolution (IDR) process steps in. But managing that process takes time, expertise, and attention to detail. That's where we come in.

Kronos Health makes IDR simple. We advocate on your behalf, prepare compelling cases, and manage every step of the arbitration process—ensuring full compliance and maximizing your reimbursement.

## How it works

1. **Submit Negotiation** — We handle the initial negotiation with the insurer and track the timeline.
2. **Initiate IDR** — If no agreement is reached, we submit the case to the federal IDR portal.
3. **Build the Case** — Our team prepares and submits a robust payment offer with full supporting documentation.
4. **Arbitration** — A neutral third-party reviews both offers and makes a binding determination.
