# About — Our Team

**URL:** https://kronoshealth.co/about/

---

Kronos Health is a forward-thinking healthcare solutions provider committed to improving clinical operations, patient outcomes, and provider reimbursement through innovative, compliant, and patient-centered programs. Our services span revenue cycle management, cognitive health integration, digital care solutions, and a national referral network for mental health support. Backed by a team with deep experience across medical billing, neuropsychology, digital health, and clinical operations, we offer practical, scalable solutions that align with modern healthcare demands. From helping providers navigate the complexities of the No Surprises Act to deploying cutting-edge tools for outcome tracking, Kronos Health delivers results that matter—for patients, providers, and practices.

## Team Members

### Juliet Mott — Director Of Operations

With over 26 years of experience in neurosurgery, Juliet brings a unique blend of clinical insight and operational leadership to her role. As Director of Operations, she is committed to ensuring every aspect of the patient journey is seamless, compassionate and efficient. Her dedication to quality care, strong leadership, and attention to detail help create a supportive environment for both patients and staff. Juliet's focus remains on providing the highest standard of care while supporting patients and staff with integrity and purpose. She remains committed to delivering exceptional service and fostering a culture of compassion, trust and professionalism.

### Heisha Rivera — Director of Revenue Cycle

A dynamic healthcare professional with over 20 years of industry experience. Expert in managing staff and overseeing patient billing processes, including appeals, coding, and follow-up. Proven success in resolving billing issues and significantly reducing claim denials. Highly knowledgeable in healthcare standards, regulatory compliance, and the No Surprise Act arbitration dispute resolution process. Skilled in meeting productivity and quality benchmarks to ensure timely, accurate reimbursement for all billed charges. Strong background in strategic business planning, process improvement, and healthcare management.

### Camila Nicasio — Revenue Cycle Specialist

Revenue Cycle Specialist at the Institute for Spine Surgery. Originally from northern New Jersey, who earned her undergraduate degree in Biology from Manhattan College in Riverdale, NY. With a strong foundation in clinical science, she transitioned into the analytical side of healthcare, where she now specializes in electronic medical record (EMR) systems and clinical revenue. Her expertise includes processing and allocating practice payments to ensure accurate and efficient financial operations within the practice.

### Nicole Cedeno — Revenue Cycle Specialist

Nicole Cedeno Teope is a Revenue Cycle Professional with over 5 years of experience in healthcare, with a diverse background spanning clinical, administrative, and surgical settings. She holds a Bachelor of Arts in Psychology from the State University of New York at Geneseo. Currently, she is a Revenue Cycle Assistant at the Institute for Spine Surgery, where she is passionate about optimizing healthcare revenue and improving financial processes to boost efficiency and sustainability. In her role, she focuses on enhancing reimbursement processes and ensuring smooth financial operations, committed to the practice's continued success.

### Jennifer Scanlon — Assistant, Operations

Jennifer is a dedicated receptionist who thrives on one-on-one interactions with patients, ensuring they feel welcomed and supported. With a background in administrative support and operations, she excels at creating efficient workflows and fostering a positive environment. Known for her detail-oriented and proactive approach, Jennifer is committed to providing exceptional service and building meaningful connections.

### Soily Rivera — Revenue Cycle Specialist

With over 22 years of experience in healthcare, Soily Rivera serves as a Revenue Specialist at New York Brain and Spine Surgery. She began her career in 2003, driven by a passion for patient advocacy, clinical innovation, and mentoring others in the field. Known for her commitment to operational efficiency and compassionate care, Soily works to ensure patients receive the support they need without unnecessary financial barriers. She continues to grow professionally while contributing to the advancement of healthcare systems and management practices.

### John Cvitkovic, P.A., P.T. — Director, Digital Health Operations

John began his career as an NYPD Detective/Sergeant, serving for 12 years until a serious injury led him to a new path. After a powerful rehab experience at NYU, he pursued a degree in Physical Therapy and later became a Physician Assistant in Orthopedics. He is a Licensed Surgical PA, Physical Therapist, and Certified Pedorthist, holding a Master's in Public Health and Physical Therapy from Mercy College. John completed residencies in Orthopedic Surgery and Pedorthics and has over 28 years of experience treating patients in New York City and the Hudson Valley. He later opened his own Physical Therapy and Orthotic & Prosthetic practice, which he sold before joining the Motion team. In his free time, he enjoys boating, gardening, beekeeping, and connecting with fellow healthcare professionals.
