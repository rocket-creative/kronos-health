## Integrated Care

Seamlessly integrate behavioral health into your care model through the Kronos Health Network.

At Kronos Health, we understand that cognitive and emotional wellness is critical—especially for high-risk patients dealing with chronic illness, injury, or pain. Our Clinically Integrated Neuropsychology program offers physicians and providers a streamlined path to refer patients for mental health evaluation and care, without disrupting their current care plan.

We've developed a collaborative network that connects primary care and specialty providers with licensed neuropsychologists and behavioral health professionals. This approach allows for real-time mental health screening and direct referral—right from your office.

## How it works

**In-Office Screening**
Use established CPT codes to administer validated cognitive or behavioral screening tools directly during the patient visit.

**Streamlined Referral Process**
Refer patients within the Kronos Health Network to our vetted mental health professionals, ensuring quick access to advanced care.

**Enhanced Patient Outcomes**
By addressing cognitive and emotional concerns early, you support improved treatment adherence, quality of life, and long-term health outcomes.

**Provider Collaboration**
We support integrated communication between referring providers and mental health specialists to ensure continuity of care and clear documentation.

## CPT Code Integration

Referring providers can submit specific CPT codes for validated screening instruments, including:

- **96127** — Brief emotional/behavioral assessment
- **96116** — Neurobehavioral status exam
- **96130–96133** — Psychological testing

These codes can be billed as part of the screening process, enhancing both clinical insight and reimbursement opportunities.

## Why it Matters

High-risk patients are often underdiagnosed when it comes to cognitive impairment, anxiety, depression, and other behavioral conditions. Our program empowers you to take action within your workflow, ensuring that no warning signs are overlooked.
