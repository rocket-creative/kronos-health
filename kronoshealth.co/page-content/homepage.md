# Smarter Healthcare. Stronger Revenue.

At Kronos Health, we bring clarity to healthcare—from billing to brain wellness.

## Explore Our Services

### Payment Dispute Resolution

Navigating the complexities of the No Surprises Act can be overwhelming for healthcare providers facing unfair out-of-network reimbursements. Our Arbitration service streamlines the Independent Dispute Resolution (IDR) process from start to finish—handling negotiation submissions, preparing compelling payment offers, and managing all federal requirements. With expert clinical insight and regulatory expertise, we maximize the likelihood of favorable outcomes and support your financial health. Let our team take the burden off your practice so you can focus on delivering exceptional care.

### Cognitive Franchise Program

Kronos Health's Cognitive Franchise Program offers a low-cost, scalable solution for treating concussions, chronic pain, and cognitive decline. With proven protocols, non-drug therapies, and tools like EEG and MRI, providers can quickly launch effective care programs. A rising franchise fee structure supports long-term growth while emphasizing medication-free cognitive health.

### Digital Health Tools

Kronos Health's Digital Health service offers turnkey tools that help providers track patient outcomes using existing CMS CPT codes across specialties like orthopedics, neurology, trauma, and cardiology. Our system handles patient enrollment, data collection, and note generation, delivering ready-to-bill records to providers. Key tools include Tappy, an SMS-based cognitive health assistant; AISA, an AI spine recovery guide; and a Digital Evaluation Module that streamlines reimbursement through automated CPT-coded reports. These solutions simplify compliance, boost efficiency, and enhance post-care engagement.

### Clinically Integrated Neuropsychology

We have developed a network for physicians who treated high-risk patients to refer to specialty providers to enhance their care program. Physicians and providers can screen patients in their office and refer to network of mental health professionals. Referring providers can submit specific CPT codes for screening instruments and then refer to a mental health specialist as part of the Kronos Health Network.
