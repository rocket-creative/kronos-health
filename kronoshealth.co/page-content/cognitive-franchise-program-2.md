## Why Cognitive Health Matters

Cognitive health has emerged as a vital pillar in long-term wellness. As of 2023, an estimated 16 million Americans over the age of 50 are living with undiagnosed cognitive dysfunction, including 7.4 million over age 65.

Risk factors for cognitive dysfunction include:
- Concussion
- Medications
- Cancer
- Obesity
- Cardiovascular disease
- Diabetes mellitus
- Anxiety and depression
- Drug or alcohol use
- Poor sleep
- Lack of physical activity

## A Scalable, Proven Program

We've developed a standardized cognitive health program based on evidence-backed protocols and clinical outcomes. Designed for quick adoption by specialist groups, this program can be launched in a short time with minimal startup costs.

A significant portion of cognitive dysfunction stems from concussions, many of which occur at work or in motor vehicle accidents. 1.7 million traumatic brain injuries occur annually in the U.S. Approximately 300,000 are due to motor vehicle accidents.

## Core Focus Areas

Our program centers on three primary diagnostic categories:
- Concussion
- Chronic Pain
- Cognitive Dysfunction

## Franchise Model

- Clinics join via a yearly franchise fee, which scales up to a baseline by year three
- Launch requires only one dedicated provider to oversee development and patient care
- Minimal startup costs, making this program ideal for fast integration into existing practices

## Clinical Approach

The goal is to deliver Neuropsychological Standard Assessments to guide therapy and treatment. For chronic pain, we emphasize non-pharmacological interventions as the cornerstone of recovery.
